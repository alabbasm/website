I enjoy learning about new tools (especially computational!) and their relation to solving problems in Chemistry and Medicine.

- Working on the [Michigan Synthetic Biology Team](https://websites.umich.edu/~umichsynbio/index.html) as the lead for computational modeling.
- I've recently started a YT channel where I make videos about math and science, check it out: [TheWondrousTurtle](https://www.youtube.com/@TheWondrousTurtle).
